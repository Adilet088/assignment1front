PGL Astana 2026

PGL Astana 2026 was an international Counter-Strike 2 tournament held in Astana, Kazakhstan. The tournament took place from May 9 to May 17, 2026 and featured 16 teams. The total prize pool was $1,600,000.

Key Dates

  1. May 9, 2026 - the tournament began.
  2. May 13, 2026 - the Swiss stage was completed.
  3. May 15, 2026 - the quarterfinals were played.
  4. May 16, 2026 - the semifinals were played.
  5. May 17, 2026 - the grand final was played.
  6. May 17, 2026 - Team Spirit won the tournament.

PGL Astana 2026 Facts

  • The tournament was held in Astana, Kazakhstan.
  • 16 teams participated in the tournament.
  • The total prize pool was $1,600,000.
  • The playoffs were played as a single-elimination bracket.
  • Team Spirit won the grand final.

Team Spirit

Team Spirit was the winner of PGL Astana 2026.

The team defeated G2 2-0 in the quarterfinals, MOUZ 2-0 in the semifinals and Falcons 3-0 in the grand final. Spirit finished the playoff stage without losing a match.

Team Spirit also had tournament MVP donk, who was one of the best-performing players at the event.

Team Spirit finished PGL Astana 2026 as tournament champions.
